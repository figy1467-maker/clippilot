ClipPilot

ClipPilot is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's own computer. It helps the owner turn their own videos into short clips and upload them to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts through each platform's official API, after the owner reviews every video.

It is not a public service: there are no user sign-ups, and it is used only with accounts the owner controls.
