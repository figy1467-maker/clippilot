Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

ClipPilot runs only on its owner's computer and is used only with the owner's own accounts.

What it uses

  • Sign-in tokens from TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, so it can upload the owner's videos. They are stored encrypted on the owner's computer and never shared.
  • Basic account info (account ID and display name) to show which account is connected.
  • The owner's videos and captions, which are sent only to the platform the owner chose.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect data about anyone else, sell data, show ads or use tracking.
  • It has no server: nothing is stored anywhere except the owner's computer and the platforms the videos go to.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect any account in the app (which revokes its TikTok login) or remove the app's access in each platform's settings at any time.

Questions: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
